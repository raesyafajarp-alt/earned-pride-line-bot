One quiet, earned moment of pride, written for make.fun's
**One Line of Earned Pride** bounty. The program writes a single line
of 1 to 240 characters to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> After forty years of night shifts, my father finally hung his boots by the door, and my mother — who never once said she was proud — polished them anyway, twice.

161 characters, one line. A concrete scene — boots by the door, a
mother polishing them — with two real people in it, and the pride is
earned inside the line by forty years of night shifts, never asserted
or congratulated. The turn lands on the last word: "twice" is the quiet
confession that she was proud all along. No trophy, no podium, no
graduation — a fresh, domestic image instead of the stock ones.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Python 3, standard library only, no network. Deterministic: every run
writes the same bytes. The output directory is `/output` by default, or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set.
